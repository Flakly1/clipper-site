Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Clipper uses, why, and how you can remove it. Clipper is a personal tool that runs on its operator's own computer; it does not have its own servers and does not sell or share personal data.

1. Information Clipper uses

When you connect an account, the platform shows you exactly what you are allowing. Depending on what you approve, Clipper may receive:

  • Login tokens that let Clipper act on the account you connected (it never sees your password).
  • Basic account information, such as the account's ID, display name and follower count.
  • Information about videos Clipper posted, such as their IDs, links, views, likes, comments, shares and average watch time.
  • Livestream content and public chat messages from channels the operator chooses to watch, used only to find moments worth clipping.

2. How it is used

  • To upload videos the operator chose to post to the operator's own connected accounts.
  • To show the operator how their posts are performing (views, followers, estimated earnings).
  • To improve which clips are suggested, based on the operator's own decisions and their posts' performance.

Clipper does not use this information for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone other than the platforms themselves when posting or reading statistics.

3. Where it is stored

All information is stored locally on the operator's own computer. Clipper communicates directly with the platforms' official services (Google/YouTube, TikTok and Meta/Instagram) to post videos and read statistics.

4. YouTube

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Clipper's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. TikTok

Clipper uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API). You can remove Clipper's access at any time in the TikTok app's settings under the apps and services you have allowed to use your account. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Instagram

Clipper uses Meta's official Instagram API. You can remove its access in your Instagram or Facebook settings under apps and websites. Meta's handling of data is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

7. Keeping and deleting information

  • Information is kept only while the account is connected and Clipper is in use.
  • Disconnecting an account (from the platform's settings, as above) stops all access; its login tokens no longer work.
  • Uninstalling Clipper deletes its stored tokens and data from the computer.
  • You can also ask for any information about you to be deleted by emailing kieranwarda52@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days.

8. Children

Clipper is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
