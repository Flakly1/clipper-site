Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool that helps its operator turn moments from livestreams into short vertical videos and post them to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It runs on the operator's own computer.

Clipper is not a public service: it is used only by its operator, with accounts the operator owns and has connected themselves. Nothing is posted unless the operator chooses to post it (or has turned on automatic posting for their own accounts).
